# Benchmark Access Control

## Authentication

- Internal tools require SSO sign-in and MFA.
- Service accounts must use scoped credentials and may not share human passwords.

## Permission Approval

- New access requests need manager approval and system owner approval.
- Temporary elevated access must include an expiry date.
- Access should follow least privilege and only cover the systems needed for the task.

## Review

- System owners review access lists every quarter.
- Accounts that are inactive for 45 days are disabled until the owner confirms a business need.
