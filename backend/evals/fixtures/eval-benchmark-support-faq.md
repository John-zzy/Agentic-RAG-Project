# Benchmark Support FAQ

## Response Times

- Standard support requests receive a first response within 1 business day.
- Urgent production incidents are triaged immediately during support hours.

## Escalation Details

- Include the affected system, impact summary, and error screenshots in the support request.
- If the issue blocks a scheduled release, mark the ticket as urgent.
- Security incidents should be routed to the security team instead of the general support queue.

## Ownership

- The support coordinator assigns the initial owner after triage.
- The owner updates the ticket when mitigation starts and when the user-visible issue is resolved.
